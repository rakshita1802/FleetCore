# One-Minute Demo Video Plan: Green Computing for Sustainable Fleet Management

**Project Title:** Green Computing for Sustainable Fleet Management (FleetCore Pro)  
**Total Target Duration:** Exactly 60 Seconds  
**Format:** Screen Recording + Voiceover / Narration  
**URL to Screen Record:** `http://localhost:5173/sustainability` (or your deployed Vercel URL)

---

## Storyboard & Timeline Breakdown

```
  0s ───────── 10s ────────────── 25s ────────────────── 40s ───────────── 52s ──────── 60s
  │  INTRO     │   DASHBOARD UI    │   HEAD-TO-HEAD COMP   │  RECOMMENDATIONS│ GREEN COMPUTING │
  │  Objective │   KPIs & Fleet    │   Diesel vs. Electric │  Idle Reduction │ CPU & Bandwidth │
```

---

### [00:00 – 00:10] Introduction: Project Title & Objective
* **Screen Visual:** Start on the FleetCore login or directly navigating to the **Green Fleet (Eco)** tab with the glowing emerald leaf icon in the sidebar. The title *"Green Computing for Sustainable Fleet Management"* is clearly visible at the top.
* **Camera Action:** Smooth mouse cursor sweep across the top header banner and Emerald badge.
* **Voiceover Script:**
  > *"Welcome to FleetCore: Green Computing for Sustainable Fleet Management. Our objective is dual-layer sustainability: cutting fleet fuel consumption and carbon emissions on the road, while cutting unnecessary server computing power in the cloud."*

---

### [00:10 – 00:25] Dashboard Overview: KPIs & Fleet Analytics
* **Screen Visual:** Zoom in on the Top KPI Ribbon showing:
  - **5,615.4 km** total distance monitored
  - **1,288.7 L** estimated fuel consumption
  - **3.42 tonnes** simulated $CO_2$ emissions
  - **4.36 km/L** fleet efficiency
  - **64.2 L** unnecessary idle waste
* **Camera Action:** Hover over the KPI cards and scroll down smoothly to the **Vehicle Sustainability & Emissions Breakdown** table. Filter the table once by clicking `Electric` or `Diesel` pill buttons.
* **Voiceover Script:**
  > *"Here on the Green Fleet Dashboard, we track real-time trip distances, fuel burn, and carbon emissions using standardized DEFRA emission factors—such as 2.68 kg of CO₂ per liter for diesel, and zero direct tailpipe emissions for electric vehicles."*

---

### [00:25 – 00:40] Sustainability Analysis: Head-to-Head Powertrain Comparison
* **Screen Visual:** Focus on the **Head-to-Head Sustainability Comparator** module.
* **Action:**
  - Select **Vehicle A:** `BharatBenz 1617R` (Diesel Heavy Commercial Truck).
  - Select **Vehicle B:** `Tata Nexon EV Max` (Electric SUV).
  - Point to the comparison card showing the drastic contrast in carbon intensity ($1,007\text{ g/km}$ vs. $0.0\text{ g/km}$).
* **Voiceover Script:**
  > *"Our interactive vehicle comparator lets managers analyze powertrains head-to-head. Comparing a heavy diesel truck with an electric vehicle reveals over five hundred kilograms of carbon saved across similar operational distances, highlighting prime routes for electrification."*

---

### [00:40 – 00:52] Eco-Driving & Operational Recommendations
* **Screen Visual:** Scroll to the **Actionable Eco-Driving & Operations** recommendation cards.
* **Action:** Hover over the orange **"High Idle Alert & Engine Shutdown Protocol"** card, showing estimated savings of **$64.2\ L$** fuel and **$172.1\ kg\ CO_2$**. Then glance at the **"Route Electrification Opportunity"** card.
* **Voiceover Script:**
  > *"FleetCore turns data into immediate action. The system flags excessive engine idling and recommends route consolidation, projecting an estimated reduction of over 170 kilograms of carbon simply by eliminating wasteful loading dock idling."*

---

### [00:52 – 01:00] Green Computing Optimization & Wrap-Up
* **Screen Visual:** Pan up to the glowing cyan/emerald **Green Computing Architecture HUD** on the right side of the screen.
* **Action:** Point out:
  - **Compute Time:** `3.2 ms` (Cache Hit)
  - **Bandwidth Saved:** `78.4%`
  - **Server CPU Cycles Saved:** `98.5%`
  - Click the **"Bust Cache"** or **"Refresh Data"** button to show the instant recomputation and cache cycle.
* **Voiceover Script:**
  > *"Best of all, FleetCore practices what it preaches: our in-memory smart caching and lean query projections cut redundant database queries, slashing server CPU runtime by 98% and network payload by 78%. Sustainable fleet, sustainable code. Thank you."*

## Presentation Checklist Before Recording
1. **Ensure Local Backend and Frontend are Running:**
   - Backend running on `http://localhost:5000`
   - Frontend running on `http://localhost:5173`
2. **Log in as Admin or Fleet Manager:**
   - Credentials: `admin@fleetcore.io` / `Admin@123`
3. **Screen Resolution:** Recommended $1920 \times 1080$ at $60\text{ FPS}$ with crisp audio.
4. **Browser State:** Set browser zoom to $100\%$ or $90\%$ so the top KPI ribbon and comparator fit cleanly on screen.
